PROJECT 3

Power BI Dashboards

Business Intelligence · Data Visualisation

BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Diabetes Risk Factor Analysis Dashboard

This academic group assignment involved using Power BI to explore health indicator data and present information through interactive visualisations and dashboard reporting.

Business Problem

Diabetes is a major health concern, making early detection and the identification of associated risk factors important.

This project investigated diabetes distribution among respondents and explored its relationship with demographic, lifestyle and health-related factors. The aim was to transform health data into meaningful insights that could support awareness, early detection and prevention.

Key Measures and Visualisations

  • Total respondents: 228,606.
  • Percentage with diabetes: 15%.
  • Average BMI: 28.51.
  • Diabetes distribution by age group and sex.
  • Diabetes counts across BMI categories.
  • Analysis of lifestyle factors, including physical activity, smoking, fruit and vegetable consumption, and heavy alcohol consumption.
  • Analysis of health conditions, including high blood pressure, high cholesterol, heart disease and stroke.
  • Diabetes patterns across income and self-rated general health categories.
  • Interactive filters for diabetes status, BMI category, sex and age group.

Key Findings

  • Approximately 15% of respondents had diabetes, while 85% did not.
  • Higher diabetes counts were observed in older age groups.
  • The obese BMI category recorded the highest diabetic count, followed by the overweight category.
  • The dashboard recorded 18,216 female and 16,685 male respondents with diabetes.
  • Diabetes status varied across lifestyle factors and other health conditions.

Recommendations

  • Promote healthy eating and weight management.
  • Encourage regular physical activity.
  • Strengthen diabetes awareness and early screening.
  • Encourage regular health checks, particularly among higher-risk groups.
  • Use data insights to guide targeted health awareness and prevention programmes.

My Learning Experience

This was a group academic assignment that helped me develop both technical and professional skills.

Working with other team members taught me the importance of teamwork, communication, accountability and sharing responsibilities. It also strengthened my understanding of how data visualisation can transform complex datasets into meaningful information that supports decision-making.

Tools: Power BI | Data Analysis | Data Visualisation
