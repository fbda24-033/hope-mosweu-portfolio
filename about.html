MY STORY

Driven by curiosity. Inspired by possibilities.

My name is Hope Changu Mosweu. I am a third-year Bachelor of Science (Honours) Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College in Gaborone, Botswana.

My academic journey has allowed me to develop an interest in the relationship between business, data and technology. I enjoy understanding how information can be collected, analysed and transformed into insights that support informed decision-making.

I am developing practical skills in Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel, databases, data visualisation and machine learning. Through academic and personal projects, I continue to strengthen my problem-solving and analytical abilities.

Beyond academics, I value leadership, teamwork, communication and continuous learning. My experience as Minister of Hostels and Cafeteria at BAC helped me appreciate the importance of listening, responsibility and representing others.

My ambition is to grow into a capable data professional, contribute to meaningful projects and use data and technology to help solve real-world problems.
